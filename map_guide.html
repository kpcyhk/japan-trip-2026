Japan Driving Quick Tips

  • Drive on the LEFT side of the road.
  • ETC Card: Ensure inserted into reader before toll gate.
  • Emergency #s: Police 110, Ambulance 119.
  • Japan Roadside Assistance (JAF): #8139.

Japan Driving Essential Info

How to use MapCodes

Select "MapCode" on your rental car's GPS unit, then key in the code (e.g. 57 094 692*80). It pins exact parking lots far better than phone numbers.

Tolls & ETC Card

Always use the purple ETC lane at toll gates if your rental includes an ETC card. Slow down to 20 km/h when entering.

Gas Stations (Refueling)

Regular gasoline is usually the RED pump nozzle (レギュラー - Regular). High octane is YELLOW, Diesel is GREEN.
